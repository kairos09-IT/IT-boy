⌨️ How to Improve Typing Fluency

Typing fluency means typing comfortably, accurately, and without constantly looking at the keyboard.

1. Learn touch typing
Try to use all your fingers instead of typing with only two fingers.
2. Focus on accuracy
Don't sacrifice accuracy for speed. Speed naturally improves with practice.
3. Practice regularly
Practice for around 10–20 minutes regularly instead of doing one huge session.
4. Don't look at the keyboard
Gradually train your fingers to remember where the keys are.
5. Track your progress
Check your WPM and accuracy from time to time.
